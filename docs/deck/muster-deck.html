A hole in the rota, four legal ways to fill it, and no time to work out which one is cheapest — or which one needs someone's permission.

So Muster does the deciding.

It drafts next week under the law, then holds it together: every lawful cover found, priced against that city's ordinance, cheapest one taken.

Every change writes a receipt naming the clause, the money, the carve-out claimed and who decided.

The rulebook is data, not code. Every figure carries the .gov source it was read from, so you can check the work without reading any Python.

Two rules ignore the dial.

Muster will never waive a worker's rest period on their behalf, and never asks a worker who has already declined.
